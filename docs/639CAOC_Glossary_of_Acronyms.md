**GLOSSARY OF ACRONYMS**

*Covers every acronym used across the 639 CAOC document set (AOC Charter 1-1, 639 CAOC TTP 1-1, the Combined Campaign Cycle and Combined AOD, the Combined Campaign Intent and its template, and the doctrinal references they draw on), plus the general DCS/real-world flight-operations, navigation, and airspace acronyms aircrew need day to day. Grouped by what the acronym is for, not alphabetically, so a new reader can learn a block at a time. An alphabetical index is at the end.*

**1. Seats and Organizations**

| Acronym | Stands for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| AOC | Air Operations Center | A large command agency that plans and conducts warfare. "639th CAOC" is this charter's specific one. |
| CAOC | Combined Air Operations Center | Same idea as AOC, with "Combined" made explicit — the multi-group layer above each group's own organization. |
| JAOC | Joint Air Operations Center | Real-world variant naming when forces of more than one Service (not necessarily more than one nation) are combined; background doctrine alongside CAOC/AOC. |
| CFC | Combined Force Commander | Top seat. Sets the combined campaign's End State and Key Tasks; reactive otherwise. Doctrinal root: JFC. |
| CFACC | Combined Force Air Component Commander | Runs the combined cycle day to day. Doctrinal root: JFACC, redesignated for combined/coalition employment. |
| JFC | Joint Force Commander | The real-world doctrinal term the CFC seat is adapted from — the appointed commander of the overall operational effort. |
| JFACC | Joint Force Air Component Commander | The real-world doctrinal term the CFACC seat is adapted from — overall responsibility for the air campaign. A participating group's own internal JFACC is called the **Component JFACC** in this document set — it answers to that group's own Campaign Commander, never to the CFACC. |
| JFLCC | Joint Forces Land Component Commander | Real-world sibling to the JFACC — overall responsibility for the ground campaign. Background doctrine; not a CAOC seat, since this project is air-focused. |
| JFMCC | Joint Forces Marine Component Commander | Real-world sibling to the JFACC — responsible for the sea warfare campaign. Background doctrine, not a CAOC seat. |
| LNO | Liaison Officer | One per participating group, sits on the AOC Board; the only person who can Buy or Pass a requirement on that group's behalf. |
| MC | Mission Commander | Plans, briefs, and leads an individual mission package within ALR and intent. |
| ACA | Airspace Control Authority | Builds and publishes the comm plan and ACO; liaison to AWACS. A function (Combat Operations/C4-IM), not necessarily a separate person. |
| AWACS | Airborne Warning and Control System | Battle-management aircraft; holds retasking authority during execution. |
| SOF | Supervisor of Flying | A qualified pilot on the ground that aircrew may contact for support and coordination while airborne — a group-level equivalent to AWACS/C2 for local-pattern work. |
| C2 | Command and Control | Functions related to and performed for control of the battlespace and/or the AOR. |
| C4/IM | Command, Control, Communications, and Computers / Information Management | The functional division that owns the Discord/DKS tooling, registration data, and Buy/Pass distribution. |
| ISR | Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance | The functional division (and doctrinal discipline) behind PIRs, the threat picture, and BDA. |
| COMAFFOR | Commander, Air Force Forces | Real-world Service-component commander role; background doctrine only, not a CAOC seat. |
| CCDR | Combatant Commander | Real-world term for the top joint commander; background doctrine. |
| JTF | Joint Task Force | Real-world term for a task-organized joint force; background doctrine. |
| AADC | Area Air Defense Commander | Real-world role, typically dual-hatted with the JFACC; background doctrine, not a separate CAOC seat. |

**2. Command Authority**

| Acronym | Stands for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| OPCON | Operational Control | Full command authority. The CAOC deliberately does *not* have this over participating groups. |
| TACON | Tactical Control | Narrower authority than OPCON; closer to what the CFACC exercises over cross-group requirements. |
| ADCON | Administrative Control | Real-world term for administrative (non-operational) authority; background doctrine. |
| COMREL | Command Relationship | Real-world general term for how authority is structured between commanders; background doctrine. |

**3. Core Products**

| Acronym | Stands for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| AOD | Air Operations Directive | "Combined AOD" at the coalition level; each group's own is its "Component AOD." Two parts: Part 1 (effects), Part 2 (guidance). |
| MAAP | Master Air Attack Plan | The overall design and schedule for the air war; at the mission level, the package-level plan each claiming group's own designers build, now largely in DKS. |
| ATO | Air Tasking Order | The final flight schedule that assigns all theater missions and targets. In this system, one draft ATO line per requirement, with Tasked Unit blank until Bought. |
| ACO | Airspace Control Order (also written Air Control Order) | The final airspace plan for the AOR/AO — tankers, CAP lanes, HVAA orbits, contact points, routing, killboxes, ROZs. Published once per campaign, reissued only on change. |
| JIPTL | Joint Integrated Prioritized Target List | The list of actionable targets in the AOR. "Combined JIPTL" at the coalition level — the clearing-house list of open requirements. Each group also keeps its own internal JIPTL for its own targets. |
| JIPCL | Joint Integrated Prioritized Collection List | Doctrinal companion to the JIPTL, for collection (ISR) priorities rather than targets; named in the sourcing, not separately built out in this document set. |
| SPINS | Special Instructions | Document that contains rules and/or procedures particular to the AOR: time/position formats, check-in rules, reporting formats, tanker rules, etc. Published once per campaign. |
| NSL | No-Strike List | Protected objects under ROE/law of armed conflict; maintained by Combat Plans. |
| RTL | Restricted Target List | Targets needing higher approval (political/cultural/friendly-ops reasons); CFC approves entries. |
| TNL | Target Nomination List | The pre-JIPTL working list of nominated targets/requirements. |
| SCL | Standard Conventional Load (also Standard Configuration Loadout) | A defined list of standard combat loads for aircraft; in the ATO checklist, a five-character loadout code (e.g., X6201, 682H3) so designers and pilots don't argue about weapons fits. |
| FragOrder (FRAGO) | Fragmentary Order | Short-notice tasking issued between scheduled cycles; the tooling assembles a draft automatically. |

**4. Risk, Rules, and Targeting Discipline**

| Acronym | Stands for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ALR | Acceptable Level of Risk | Guidance for aircrew to provide appropriate levels of aggressiveness in air operations. LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH / EXTREME. The CFACC sets a coalition *floor*; a group may tighten it, never loosen it without approval. |
| ROE | Rules of Engagement | A set of legal rules that must not be violated for numerous legal and/or political reasons. WEAPONS FREE / TIGHT / HOLD by area; also a coalition floor here. |
| SROE | Standing Rules of Engagement | The real-world permissive baseline (CJCSI 3121.01B) the DCS ROE structure is modeled on. |
| CDE | Collateral Damage Estimate (criteria) | Low/Elevated/High approval tiers tied to the dial sheet's ROE/collateral-sensitivity setting. |
| TST | Time-Sensitive Target | Pre-empts other tasking when found; published in its own matrix with accepted risk. |
| CCIR | Commander's Critical Information Requirement | Umbrella term for PIR + FFIR — the questions that drive a commander's decisions. |
| PIR | Priority Intelligence Requirement | What must be confirmed about the enemy/environment before planning proceeds. |
| FFIR | Friendly Force Information Requirement | The same idea, but about your own forces (sign-ups, tanker/AWACS crewing). |
| LTIOV | Latest Time Information of Value | The deadline by which a PIR/FFIR answer stops being useful. |
| EOB | Enemy Order of Battle | The threat picture ISR maintains and rates by confidence (H/M/L). |
| MOE | Measure of Effectiveness | Did the *effect* happen (operational-level question)? |
| MOP | Measure of Performance | Did the *mission* execute as tasked (tactical-level question)? |
| OO | Operational Objective | Doctrinal term behind "End State" in the strategy-to-task chain. |
| TO | Tactical Objective | Doctrinal term behind "this cycle's effect." |
| TT | Tactical Task | Doctrinal term behind an individual target/requirement. |

**5. Tactics, Missions, and Threat Terms**

| Acronym | Stands for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| SEAD | Suppression of Enemy Air Defenses | |
| OCA | Offensive Counter Air | A set of missions that are offensive in nature, used to project air power forward. |
| DCA | Defensive Counter Air | A set of missions that are defensive in nature, used to defend an area. |
| CAS | Close Air Support | Controlled fires within a defined range of friendly assets; can only occur between the FLOT and the FSCL. Key Task 5 in the Combined Campaign Intent — continuous, on-call, no target tier of its own. |
| CAP | Combat Air Patrol | An area where friendly air power will engage enemy aircraft and prevent incursions. Defensive in nature. |
| CSAR | Combat Search and Rescue | |
| IADS | Integrated Air Defense System | Tracked as a dial-sheet setting (density/posture). |
| SAM | Surface-to-Air Missile | |
| AAA | Anti-Aircraft Artillery | |
| MANPADS | Man-Portable Air-Defense System | |
| EWR | Early Warning Radar | |
| GCI | Ground-Controlled Intercept | |
| AIC | Airborne Intercept Control | A designated frequency or channel used for Command and Control of aircraft intercepts. |
| FLOT | Forward Line of Own Troops | The most forward line beyond which there are no friendly troops. |
| FSCL | Fire Support Coordination Line | The farthest range line where indigenous ground fires (artillery, MLRS, etc.) can reach; bounds CAS together with the FLOT. |
| WEZ | Weapons Engagement Zone | |
| MEZ | Missile Engagement Zone | |
| ROZ | Restricted Operating Zone | A zone that may not be penetrated due to artillery, MLRS, UAV ops, etc. |
| LOC | Line(s) of Communication | Key Task 2 target focus (bridges, rail, roads). **Caution:** also used for Localizer in navigation contexts — see Section 14. |
| MSR | Main Supply Route | |
| HVT | High-Value Target | A ground/fixed target whose loss is especially damaging to the enemy. |
| HVAA | High-Value Airborne Asset | Any airborne asset that is a force-multiplier, the loss of which would be grave to the effort. |
| BVR | Beyond Visual Range | Engagements that do not meet visual criteria. |
| BFM | Basic Fighter Maneuvering | Close-in fighter engagements. |
| TOT | Time on Target | |
| ETA | Estimated Time of Arrival | |
| TOD | Time of Day | A common signal for alignment of HAVE QUICK radio sets. |
| HPRF | High Pulse Repetition Frequency | Radar mode term. |
| MPRF | Medium Pulse Repetition Frequency | Radar mode term — note this abbreviation is reused for Multi-Point Refueling System (Section 11); context decides which. |
| BLUFOR | Blue Force (friendly side) | Placed in the initial miz in the DCS Mission Editor before the mission moves to DKS for detailed planning. |
| REDFOR | Red Force (opposing side) | Same as above — the enemy order of battle placed in the initial miz. |

**10. DCS / Community-Specific Tools**

| Acronym | Stands for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| DCS | Digital Combat Simulator | The game/sim this whole project supports. A mission (the .miz file) is first built in the stock DCS Mission Editor — BLUFOR/REDFOR placed per the theater and the designer's goals — then uploaded to DKS for detailed flight-level planning. |
| DKS | Digital Kneeboard Simulator | A member-built (JuiceBox) mission-planning web app — "DCS Mission Editor on steroids." Takes the designer's .miz and lets flights plan in detail: waypoints, the comms plan, and unit coordination. Features include Discord integration and Tacview debrief integration. Outputs: updated .miz files, DTC uploads for jets, and a full set of customizable kneeboards. This is the tool Charter Section 5.5 and Annex B's C4/Information Management division is built around. |
| MIZ | Mission file (DCS's native mission-file format, .miz) | The base mission built in the DCS Mission Editor; DKS reads and updates it. |
| SRS | SimpleRadio Standalone | The voice-comms tool named in the comm matrix. |
| DTC | Data Cartridge | DKS's output that loads mission/waypoint/comm data directly onto a jet's systems, replacing manual cockpit data entry. |
| Tacview | *(product name, not an acronym)* | Third-party flight-data-recorder/debrief tool; DKS integrates with it for post-mission debrief and BDA confirmation. |

**11. Air Refueling**

| ARCT | Air Refueling Contact Point (coordination reference) | A reference to contact the tanker for rejoin coordination — same underlying point as ARCP, named for the radio call. |

**12. Airspace, Range, and Battlespace Management**

| Acronym | Stands for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| AO / AOR | Area of Operation / Area of Operational Responsibility | The area of the defined battlespace in which we operate and have primary responsibility for. |
| JOA | Joint Operations Area | An area where multi-service operations take place. |
| BMA | Battle Management Area | A defined area for combat operations / area of responsibility for a C2 agency. |
| ATCAA | Air Traffic Control Assigned Airspace | Airspace set aside for use by military aircraft for maneuvering, 18,000 ft and above. |
| MIRC | Mariana Islands Range Complex | A real-world training range; appears in DCS as a terrain/range reference. |
| NTTR | Nevada Test and Training Range | Same — a real-world training range used as a DCS map/reference. |
| SCF | Squadron Common Frequency | A designated frequency each squadron uses for interplane communication. |

**13. Airfield and Ground Operations**

| Acronym | Stands for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ATIS | Air Terminal Information Service | A recording of the current observed local weather conditions at the airport. |
| CTAF | Common Traffic Advisory Frequency | A common frequency designated per airport for coordination and flow of traffic. |
| ATC | Air Traffic Control | Any agency designed to conduct the flow of air traffic. |
| ARTCC | Air Route Traffic Control Center ("Center") | An agency to expedite the flow of air traffic from point to point. |
| SOF | Supervisor of Flying | See Section 1 — a ground-based pilot aircrew can contact for support/coordination. |
| LOLA | Live Ordnance Loading Area | An area designated for loading of live munitions. |
| EOR | End of Runway | An area where fighter aircraft perform checks and arm/de-arm live ordnance. |
| VFR | Visual Flight Rules | Flight using primary visual reference to the ground. |
| IFR | Instrument Flight Rules | A set of procedures and rules for flying without visible reference to the ground. |
| FCLP | Field Carrier Landing Procedure | A procedure to simulate and practice carrier landings at land airports. |

**14. Navigation and Approach Procedures**

| Acronym | Stands for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| TACAN | Tactical Air Navigation | A portable beacon used for homing to or from, both ground and air. Also appears as an ATO data element (tanker TACAN channel). |
| DME | Distance Measuring Equipment | An avionics suite that precisely measures slant range using radio signals. |
| ILS | Instrument Landing System | A set of transmitters and receivers to guide an aircraft to a runway for landing. |
| LOC (Localizer) | Localizer | A ground-based navigation facility that provides lateral guidance only to the runway — part of an ILS. **Not** the same LOC as Line(s) of Communication (Section 5); context decides which is meant. |
| ICLS | Instrument Carrier Landing System | An avionics system for instrument approaches to the boat. |
| ACLS | Automatic Carrier Landing System | An avionics system specific to the F/A-18 for automatic approach and landing on the carrier. |
| IP | Initial Point | A reference point to begin a particular action. |
| IAF | Initial Approach Fix | A fix on an approach where the procedure may start. |
| IF | Intermediate Fix | A fix on an approach that defines the intermediate section (that section prior to the final approach section). |
| FAF | Final Approach Fix | That section of an approach where runway alignment is complete and letdown to the runway begins. |
| MAP | Missed Approach Point | That point on an approach where landing is not possible and a go-around must begin. |
| MDA | Minimum Descent Altitude | The minimum altitude on a non-precision approach that you may descend to. |
| DH / DA | Decision Height / Decision Altitude | That point on an approach where the landing/go-around decision must be made. |
| TDZE | Touchdown Zone Elevation | The elevation where the airplane will touch down on the runway. |
| MSA | Minimum Safe Altitude | A minimum altitude that assures terrain obstruction clearance within the defined area. |
| GS | Ground Speed | |
