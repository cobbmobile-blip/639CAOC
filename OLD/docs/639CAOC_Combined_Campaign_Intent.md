**CHARTER: Authority**

This Intent governs what **crosses group lines**. It does not reach into how any participating group runs its own internal cycle (Charter Section 8). **No tasking under it is binding on a group without that group's LNO concurring** (Charter Sections 4 and 5.3). The CFC and CFACC seats are a service rotation, not a position of authority over the coalition (Section 2.3).

**Constraints and Risk**

- **Planned strike and CAS are not committed against an undegraded IADS.**

- **Troops-in-contact CAS is a standing time-sensitive target, pre-authorized at ALR HIGH.** It interrupts whichever air phase is being worked.

- **The ALR and ROE below are a coalition floor** the CFACC sets and publishes in Combined AOD Part 2. A group may set a tighter (lower-risk) standard internally. It may not set a looser one without CFACC approval (Charter Section 10, Part 2).

- **Force preservation is a constraint, not a task.** The loss tripwire below forces a pause when losses exceed what the coalition can absorb.

- **The ROE / collateral sensitivity dial** (Charter Annex C.4) sets how strict collateral damage criteria are this cycle.

**Air Phases, Weight of Effort, and ALR**

Each air phase has a **standing weight of effort**. It does not need to be reinvented every cycle. The CFACC chooses the current air phase and may **move any category up to the apportionment swing threshold** (default **15 points**, set by the CFC in Campaign Mechanics below) with a one-line reason in the Combined AOD. Changes must total 100%. A larger swing goes to the CFC first. Combined AOD Part 2 then splits the result **by participating group**. Percentages apply to the **sorties actually signed up** across groups.

| **Air phase** | **Main effort** | **Standing weight of effort** | **Default ALR** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Air Phase 1: Air Superiority (contested)** | Task 1 | CAP/Sweep 45% • SEAD 30% • Strike (airfields/IADS) 20% • CAS/Other 5% | SEAD HIGH; strikers MEDIUM |
| **Air Phase 2: LOC Interdiction** | Task 2 | Strike (LOCs) 45% • CAP (maintain) 25% • SEAD (residual/reactive) 15% • CAS 15% | MEDIUM |
| **Air Phase 3: C2 Targeting** | Task 3 | Strike (C2) 40% • CAP 20% • SEAD 15% • CAS 25% | MEDIUM |
| **Air Phase 4: Rear Echelon Attrition** | Task 4 | Strike (rear echelon) 40% • CAS 30% • CAP 20% • SEAD 10% | MEDIUM; HIGH for A-grade |

- **CAS is a floor, not a cap.** A troops-in-contact call can pull sorties from another category mid-cycle.

- **Air superiority is never won permanently.** Keep a standing CAP/sweep floor (20 to 25% in Air Phases 2 to 4) so Red cannot contest it back.

**Transition and reversion rules**

- **Review limit (Options 1 and 2):** if an air phase is not met in **4 cycles**, the CFACC briefs the CFC and recommends: raise ALR, add assets, or accept the criteria as met. Option 3 allows one extension cycle per air phase.

- **Reversion trigger:** the main effort returns to Task 1 automatically if the IADS rebuilds above the Air Phase 1 threshold (Option 1: above 20% of baseline; Option 2: a named battery is back in action; Option 3: a SAM-caused loss in the corridor), or if the loss tripwire is breached.

- **Overlap:** the CFC may approve an air phase started early while Air Phase 1 criteria stay held.

**How This Drives the Combined JIPTL**

This Intent does not change cycle to cycle. What changes is the Combined JIPTL. Each cycle, the CFACC's Combat Plans division sorts that cycle's confirmed requirements by **Key Task (1 to 4)** and assigns each a **grade A to D** for urgency (A: must happen this cycle; B: better now; C: nice to have; D: backup). Generally do not spend sorties on a lower task until the one above is sufficiently suppressed for that cycle. **Task 5 is not sorted into a task.** It is continuous and interrupts whichever task is being worked when there is a troops-in-contact call.

|---|---|
| **Task 1: Air Superiority** | SA-11 battery at XX5566 (blocks strike ingress); Red fighter squadron at Northvale Airbase (active CAP threat); EWR site at XX1234. |
| **Task 2: LOCs** | Bridge at XX3344 (main resupply route); rail yard at Eastmark. |
| **Task 3: C2** | Comms relay at XX5577; brigade HQ near Westfield. |
| **Task 4: Rear Echelon** | Ammo depot at XX7788; maintenance depot near Ridgeport. |

*A target can outrank its task if it directly threatens Task 1. For example, a SAM site that is "rear echelon" by type is treated as Task 1 if it covers the strike corridor. All place names here are notional.*
